# Middle East Economy & Oil Prices Analysis (1990-2024)
📊 Project Overview
A comprehensive data science analysis exploring the relationship between crude oil prices and economic performance across 13 Middle Eastern countries over 34 years (1990-2024). This project examines how oil-dependent and diversified economies have responded to oil price fluctuations, providing actionable insights for economic planning and investment decisions.

🎯 Objectives\n\n- Analyze the correlation between Brent crude oil prices and key economic indicators\n- Compare economic performance between oil-exporting and diversified economies\n- Identify historical trends, outliers, and patterns across the region\n- Quantify the impact of oil price shocks on GDP growth and inflation\n- Develop interactive visualization dashboards for economic monitoring

📁 Dataset
The dataset contains **455 records** covering **13 countries** with the following key indicators:| Column | Description |\n|--------|-------------| Country | 13 Middle Eastern nations || Year | 1990-2024 | GDP_current_USD | Gross Domestic Product (current USD) | GDP_growth_annual_pct | Annual GDP growth rate | GDP_per_capita_current_USD | GDP per capita | Inflation_consumer_prices_annual_pct | Consumer price inflation | Exports_pct_GDP | Exports as percentage of GDP | Imports_pct_GDP | Imports as percentage of GDP | FDI_net_inflows_pct_GDP | Foreign Direct Investment inflows |\n| Unemployment_total_pct | Total unemployment rate | Life_expectancy_years | Life expectancy at birth | Brent_Oil_Price_USD_per_barrel | Annual Brent crude price |
**Countries Analyzed:**- Bahrain, Egypt, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Yemen
