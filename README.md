# Middle East Economy & Oil Prices Analysis (1990-2024)\n\n## 📊 Project Overview\n\nA comprehensive data science analysis exploring the relationship between crude oil prices and economic performance across 13 Middle Eastern countries over 34 years (1990-2024). This project examines how oil-dependent and diversified economies have responded to oil price fluctuations, providing actionable insights for economic planning and investment decisions.\n\n## 🎯 Objectives\n\n- Analyze the correlation between Brent crude oil prices and key economic indicators\n- Compare economic performance between oil-exporting and diversified economies\n- Identify historical trends, outliers, and patterns across the region\n- Quantify the impact of oil price shocks on GDP growth and inflation\n- Develop interactive visualization dashboards for economic monitoring\n\n## 📁 Dataset\n\nThe dataset contains **455 records** covering **13 countries** with the following key indicators:\n\n| Column | Description |\n|--------|-------------|\n| Country | 13 Middle Eastern nations |\n| Year | 1990-2024 |\n| GDP_current_USD | Gross Domestic Product (current USD) |\n| GDP_growth_annual_pct | Annual GDP growth rate |\n| GDP_per_capita_current_USD | GDP per capita |\n| Inflation_consumer_prices_annual_pct | Consumer price inflation |\n| Exports_pct_GDP | Exports as percentage of GDP |\n| Imports_pct_GDP | Imports as percentage of GDP |\n| FDI_net_inflows_pct_GDP | Foreign Direct Investment inflows |\n| Unemployment_total_pct | Total unemployment rate |\n| Life_expectancy_years | Life expectancy at birth |\n| Brent_Oil_Price_USD_per_barrel | Annual Brent crude price |\n\n**Countries Analyzed:**\n- Bahrain, Egypt, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Yemen\n\n## 🛠️ Technologies Used\n\n| Category | Tools |\n|----------|-------|\n| **Languages** | Python 3.13 |\n| **Data Processing** | pandas, numpy |\n| **Visualization** | matplotlib, seaborn, plotly |\n| **Machine Learning** | scikit-learn (Random Forest) |\n| **Environment** | Jupyter Notebook |\n\n## 📈 Key Analyses Performed\n\n### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis\n- Missing value analysis and imputation using linear interpolation\n- Descriptive statistics by country and decade\n- Outlier detection using the IQR (Interquartile Range) method\n- Comprehensive correlation matrix of all economic indicators\n\n### 2. Oil Price Impact Analysis\n- Year-over-year oil price change calculations\n- Correlation between oil prices and GDP growth across different country types\n- Regional average growth by decade heatmap\n- High vs. low oil price period comparisons\n- Scatter plots with trend lines for key relationships\n\n### 3. Country-Specific Deep Dives\n- Individual economic performance timelines with dual-axis plots\n- Comparison between oil-exporters vs. diversified economies\n- Conflict period impact analysis (Iraq War, Arab Spring, Lebanon Crisis, Yemen Conflict)\n- Examination of extreme economic events and their causes\n\n### 4. Predictive Modeling\n- Random Forest Regression for GDP growth prediction\n- Feature importance analysis to identify key economic drivers\n- Time series preparation for forecasting\n\n## 📊 Key Findings\n\n| Finding | Insight |\n|---------|---------|\n| **Oil Price Correlation** | Strong positive correlation (0.32) between oil prices and GDP per capita, particularly pronounced in oil-exporting nations |\n| **Inflation Impact** | Oil-exporters experienced significantly lower inflation during high-price periods (6.94% vs 16.74% average in diversified economies) |\n| **GDP Growth** | Oil-exporters showed 5.44% average GDP growth during high-price periods compared to 4.15% during low-price periods |\n| **Diversified Economies** | Demonstrated more stable growth patterns (~4.0% on average) regardless of oil price fluctuations |\n| **Life Expectancy** | Moderate positive correlation (0.40) between oil wealth and life expectancy improvements |\n| **Regional Disparity** | Significant variation between Gulf nations (high GDP per capita) and conflict-affected countries |\n| **GDP Share** | Saudi Arabia leads the region with ~$1.24 trillion GDP, followed by UAE, Israel, Iran, and Egypt |\n\n## 📊 Visualizations\n\nThe project includes multiple visualization outputs:\n\n1. **GDP Analysis Dashboard** - Total GDP trends, growth rates, per capita metrics with oil price overlay\n2. **Oil Price Impact Dashboard** - Price timeline, correlation scatter plots with regression lines, decade-based heatmaps\n3. **Correlation Matrix** - Comprehensive heatmap of all 12 economic indicators\n4. **Outlier Detection** - Box plots for identifying extreme values across all variables\n5. **Country Deep Dives** - Individual country performance with conflict period highlighting\n6. **Decade Analysis** - Average GDP growth by decade visualized as heatmaps\n\n## 🚀 How to Run\n\n### Prerequisites\n\n```bash\npip install pandas numpy matplotlib seaborn plotly scikit-learn\n```\n\n### Execution\n\n1.
