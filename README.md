## Overview

This project analyzes Middle Eastern economic performance and oil-price dynamics from 1990 to 2024, combining exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, visualization, and machine learning to uncover long-term trends and economic relationships.

The analysis examines oil-price movements, country-level economic indicators, correlations, and similarities between economies.

## Objectives

* Analyze long-term oil-price trends and volatility.
* Compare economic performance across Middle Eastern countries.
* Examine relationships between oil prices and economic indicators.
* Identify similarities between economies using machine learning.
* Translate historical data into actionable economic insights.

## Methodology

The workflow includes:

Data Cleaning → EDA → Statistical Analysis → Visualization → Feature Engineering → Clustering → Insights

Pandas and NumPy were used for data preparation and analysis, while Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly supported visual storytelling. Scikit-learn was used for machine-learning-based pattern discovery and clustering.

## Key Insights

* Oil prices experienced substantial long-term volatility, with major market disruptions creating significant fluctuations across the 1990–2024 period.
* Economic performance varied considerably across countries, highlighting differences in economic structure and exposure to the energy sector.
* Oil-price movements showed measurable relationships with economic indicators, reinforcing the importance of energy-market conditions when analyzing oil-dependent economies.
* Clustering revealed groups of countries with similar economic characteristics, demonstrating that Middle Eastern economies do not follow a single economic pattern.
* Economic diversification matters: countries with broader economic structures can exhibit different performance patterns from economies more heavily dependent on oil-related activity.

Note: Correlation indicates association, not causation. The findings should therefore be interpreted within the broader macroeconomic and geopolitical context.
